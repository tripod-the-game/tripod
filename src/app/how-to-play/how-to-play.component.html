How to Play

Objective

Fill three words into a triangle. All three words share the same category.

The Triangle

  • Left edge — Word 1 (reads bottom to top)
  • Right edge — Word 2 (reads top to bottom)
  • Bottom row — Word 3 (reads left to right)

Shared Corners

  • Top: Last letter of Word 1 = First letter of Word 2
  • Bottom-left: First letter of Word 1 = First letter of Word 3
  • Bottom-right: Last letter of Word 2 = Last letter of Word 3

Feedback Colors

Green — Correct position
Yellow — Valid word, wrong slot
Red flash — No correct letters
