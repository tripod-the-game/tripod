Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

No Accounts, No Sign-In

Tripod doesn't require you to create an account or sign in. There's no personal profile, and we don't collect your name, email, or contact information through the app itself.

What's Stored on Your Device

Your game progress, statistics, hints used, and past submissions are stored locally on your device using browser/app local storage. This data stays on your device and isn't sent to us.

Usage Analytics

We use Vercel Analytics to understand how the app is used (such as page views and general usage patterns). This is aggregated usage data and isn't linked to your identity.

Puzzle Content

The app fetches daily puzzle data from a public GitHub repository. No personal information is sent as part of these requests.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, email us at tripodsupport@gmail.com.
