Tripod is a daily word puzzle where three words share a triangle's corners — solve all three from a single category clue.

HOW IT WORKS
Two words run down the left and right edges of a triangle, and a third runs along the bottom. Wherever two words meet at a corner, they share a letter. Fill in every circle to complete all three words at once.

Get instant, color-coded feedback on every guess:
• Green — that letter is in the exact right spot
• Purple — you spelled one of the puzzle's words, just in the wrong slot
• Yellow — that letter belongs somewhere in the puzzle, just not there
Guess as many times as you want — there's no limit, and no penalty for trying.

STUCK? YOU'VE GOT HELP
Three hints per puzzle reveal a random letter when you need a nudge. Can't crack it? Reveal the full answer and jump into tomorrow's puzzle with a clean slate.

BUILD A STREAK
A new puzzle unlocks every day at midnight. Track your win percentage, current streak, and guess distribution in your stats, and share your results with friends when you solve it.

CATCH UP ANYTIME
Missed a day? Browse past puzzles and play any date you haven't finished yet.

No ads interrupting your puzzle. No pressure — just three words, one triangle, and a category to crack.
